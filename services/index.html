Six services cover the whole job, from pulling out standing water to rebuilding the wall. Pick the one that matches what you see, or call and we will help sort it out.

Water damage restoration is not one task. It is a chain of stages, and each stage has its own methods and its own risks. A flooded kitchen needs extraction first. A ceiling stain needs the source found. A musty closet may need mold work. The services below match those stages so you can find the right one quickly.

Most jobs in Laguna Woods use more than one service. Water mitigation follows emergency water removal, and repair follows drying. If you call the 24/7 line, we plan the sequence with you so nothing is done out of order.

How the services fit together

Think of the work in four phases. Phase one is stopping the water and removing what is standing. Phase two is drying the structure with air movers and dehumidifiers. Phase three is cleaning and removing materials that cannot be saved. Phase four is repair.

The emergency water removal and water mitigation pages cover phases one and two. Water damage cleanup covers phase three, and water damage repair covers phase four. Flood damage restoration and mold remediation apply when the water came from outside or moisture has already led to mold growth.

Doing the phases in order protects your investment. Rebuilding before drying is complete is the most common way a repair fails.
