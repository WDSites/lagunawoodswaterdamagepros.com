About Laguna Woods Water Damage Pros

We are a water damage restoration service based at 24270 El Toro Road in Laguna Woods. Here is how we work and what we will and will not promise.

24/7 Emergency Dispatch(949) 670-0939Call Now

Laguna Woods Water Damage Pros is a water damage restoration service for Laguna Woods and nearby Orange County towns. We handle emergency water removal, drying, cleanup, mold work and repair. The business is set up around one idea: when water is spreading, the first thing people need is a person who answers the phone.

That is why the number on every page is a single 24/7 line, (949) 670-0939. You will not find a contact form on this site, because a form cannot send a crew. A phone call can.

How we work

We start by finding out what happened. Where did the water come from, what has it touched and is the source stopped? Those three answers decide whether the first step is extraction, drying, cleanup or something else.

Before work starts, the crew explains the scope and the price. You hear what will be done, what it will cost and why. If the scope changes because the crew finds more wet material, the change is explained before it happens.

We follow the published industry standards for the work. The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard covers professional water damage restoration, and the S520 standard covers professional mold remediation. Those standards are the reference points, and we use moisture readings to show when drying is done.

Why Laguna Woods

Laguna Woods has a housing mix that most restoration companies do not see every day. Much of the city is Laguna Woods Village, built in phases from 1963 to 1985, where manors share walls and ceilings and where community rules affect repairs. A leak in one unit often reaches another.

Knowing that changes how a job runs. We ask early who else is affected, whether Resident Services has been notified and what approvals the work might need. Those questions save time and avoid trouble with neighbors and the mutual.

Our address is 24270 El Toro Road, Laguna Woods, CA 92637, and crews serve the city and the neighboring towns from there.

What we will not promise

We do not promise arrival times, because traffic, weather and call volume change them. We aim for same-day response when available, and the dispatcher tells you what to expect on the call.

We do not promise insurance approval. Your policy and your insurer decide coverage. What we do is document the damage with photos, moisture readings and a written scope, so you have good records to share.

We do not diagnose health effects of mold, and we do not claim mold will never return. Mold needs moisture, so the lasting fix is correcting the moisture source, and we say so plainly.

What a typical call looks like

A caller might say a ceiling in the second-floor bedroom has a brown stain and is dripping. The dispatcher asks whether the water is still coming, whether anyone upstairs has been reached and whether the light fixture is wet. Those answers shape the first instructions and the crew's equipment list.

Another caller might have a washing machine hose that failed while no one was home. In that case the questions turn to how long the water ran, how far it traveled and whether it reached a neighbor. The longer the water ran, the more likely it soaked the pad and the wall base, and the more important moisture readings become.

In both cases, the first minutes of the call are about safety and stopping the source. Planning the repair comes later, after the structure is dry.

Who to call for what

Call us for water that needs to come out, structures that need to dry, materials that need to be removed and walls that need to be rebuilt. For a gas smell, a fire or an electrical emergency, call 911 first. For a leak inside a Village manor, also report it to Resident Services at 949-597-4600.
