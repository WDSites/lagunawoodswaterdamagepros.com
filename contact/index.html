What to have ready when you call

A short checklist

  • The address where the water is, and the unit or floor if it is a stacked building.
  • Where the water came from and whether it has been shut off.
  • What the water has reached, such as carpet, walls, ceilings, cabinets or electrical outlets.
  • Whether the water looks clean, cloudy or dirty, or has an odor.
  • Your insurer's name, if you have it handy. You do not need your claim number to call.

Before you call

If you smell gas, see smoke or think water has reached live electrical equipment, call 911 first. If you live in Laguna Woods Village, report the leak to Resident Services at 949-597-4600 as well. Then call us for extraction, drying and repair.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.
